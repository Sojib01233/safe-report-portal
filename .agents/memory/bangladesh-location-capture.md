---
name: Bangladesh location capture
description: Product decision for collecting report geography in Bangladesh without pretending to maintain an exhaustive village directory.
---

Use structured selectors for Bangladesh divisions and districts, then let reporters enter their police station/upazila and village, ward, or area as text.

**Why:** Division and district lists are stable enough for guided selection, while police-station boundaries and village-level names are too broad and changeable for a small embedded directory; free text also covers urban wards and neighbourhoods.

**How to apply:** Keep all four location values required for a report, normalize the combined display location on the server, and only add a larger thana/union directory if the product is ready to own and maintain that dataset.
